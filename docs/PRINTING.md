# Printing and physical dimensions

## Editable designs

Canvas dimensions are stored in millimetres, with sides from **10 to 2500 mm**. PNG and flattened one-page PDF rendering use physical dimensions and a chosen resolution. Common 150/300-dpi choices and explicit lower-DPI choices are offered when they fit. The raster is capped at **16 million pixels and 8192 pixels per side**; even the lowest offered DPI may not fit every huge page. Lower DPI cannot add image detail. Placed-image effective DPI accounts for the actual cover crop; a warning asks before export when detail may be insufficient. PDF text is flattened, not searchable/editable.

Printing sends the generated PDF and page format through the OS print handoff. It does not determine printer hardware support. Print Studio also provides an A4 check PDF with a 100-mm measurement line.

## Photo sheets

Finished-photo dimensions are separate from the selected physical sheet/media. Choose a catalogue size or custom page sides **20–2500 mm**, frame sides **5–250 mm**, margins **0–100 mm**, spacing **0–50 mm** and **1–100 copies** that fit on one page. The same physical media catalogue is shared with other design/scan/Office paths where applicable. Images are contained within the frame, not automatically face-cropped. Stock/finish and optional GSM are recorded as production notes in the PDF; they do not control the printer driver.

Jurisdiction-specific profiles are source-scoped guidance, not a compliance checker. Tanzania e-Visa upload dimensions/size are not a Tanzania passport print rule; ICAO portrait-zone guidance is not a universal submitted-photo ratio. US and Canadian paper-photo requirements include restrictions on editing/printing. The sheet tool does **not** create or validate a digital upload file. Always check current authority instructions independently.

## Verify output

Choose a new save destination to protect the source. Print at actual size/100% when available, and measure a test print: drivers may scale or clip and may not support a chosen feed, stock or borderless mode. Colour management, CMYK, bleed/crop marks, production queues and hardware discovery are not established. Structural/host tests do not prove the physical printer, margins, colour or device dialog; all need real-device verification.
