The [Pub notice collection](third_party/pub-packages.LICENSES.txt) contains root-level licence/notice files for all 87 hosted packages in `pubspec.lock`, including development and platform-specific dependencies. In particular it includes `image_picker_android` 0.8.13+25, `image_picker_platform_interface` 2.11.2, `flutter_plugin_android_lifecycle` 2.0.35 and `cross_file` 0.3.5+5. Recreate it from the local Pub cache with `python3 tools/generate_pub_notices.py` after resolving packages and review the diff. Some packages do not ship on every platform.

**This is not a complete binary licence audit.** It does not establish all nested notices, Flutter engine, Android Gradle/AAR/OS, desktop native or CocoaPods obligations. Flutter's generated licence registry includes package notices; inspect the actual merged dependencies, permissions, bundled assets and native binaries for every distribution. The Gradle wrapper scripts carry Apache-2.0 headers; distribution URL and SHA-256 are pinned in `android/gradle/wrapper/gradle-wrapper.properties`.

## Branding and samples

- The D+S mark and Android derivatives were created for this project; source and regeneration are documented in [brand assets](docs/engineering/BRAND_ASSETS.md). No third-party logo or trademark is used as the launcher identity.
- Fictional example pages were generated by `tools/generate_practice_documents.py` using DejaVu Sans glyphs; the [DejaVu font notice](third_party/dejavu-font-copyright.txt) is included. The editable design contains example text, not a bundled font. The desk and garden photographs are generated synthetic images; the noisy garden is a deterministic alteration of the synthetic clean base. They do not depict real documents or people. No personal documents are bundled.
- **Bundled recognition assets:** the offline OCR models in `assets/tessdata/`
  (`eng.traineddata` 4.1 MB, `swa.traineddata` 2.2 MB) come from the Apache-2.0
  `tessdata_fast` repository and are shipped inside the app so recognition works
  without a download. The engine is Tesseract 5.5.1 through Tesseract4Android
  4.9.0 (Apache-2.0) resolved from JitPack. No model is downloaded at runtime
  and no image is uploaded.
- **Embedded font:** `assets/fonts/NotoSans-*.ttf` (OFL-1.1) is embedded in
  generated PDFs. The OFL requires the licence text to accompany the font; it is
  reproduced at [third_party/noto-fonts.OFL.txt](third_party/noto-fonts.OFL.txt).
- **No LibreOffice engine is bundled.** It is an optional external engine; see
  [office support](docs/engineering/OFFICE_SUPPORT.md).
